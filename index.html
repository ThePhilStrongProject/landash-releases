What it does

Your whole network, at a glance.

Everything runs on the dongle itself. Nothing to install on your computer, nothing to sign up for.

Finds every device

A gentle sweep every few minutes, plus the names devices announce for themselves. It tells phones from TVs from printers, and shows who's new.

Knows what each one runs

A slow background port scan finds the web pages, file shares and services on each device, and tells you when one appears or goes away.

One home for your services

Pin your router, NAS and smart-home pages to a dashboard that checks each one answers, and keep their logins beside them, locked with your own passphrase.

Private by design

Your device list stays on the dongle. It only reaches out to set its clock, check the internet is up, and fetch its own updates, which install themselves.

A closer look

Built to be left running.

Open it from any browser on your network at landash.local. The 1.14" screen on the dongle shows its address.

Get started

Up and running in five minutes.

Install straight from this page. After that, your dongle keeps itself up to date.

  • A Waveshare ESP32-C6-GEEK
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer
  • A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network
  1. Plug the dongle into your computer

    Straight into a USB port, or with a USB-A extension lead.

  2. Install

    Press Install, pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list, and confirm. It takes about a minute.

  3. Connect it to your Wi-Fi

    The dongle's screen shows a Wi-Fi network called LANDA-XXXX and its password. Join it, open http://192.168.4.1, and pick your home Wi-Fi.

  4. Open your dashboard

    Plug it in anywhere you like. The screen shows its address, or just go to http://landash.local.
